This is my first reasonably high resolution [Gigapan](http://gigapan.org/). It is around 760 megapixel. It was a dark day on Saturday but I am quite happy with the results. You can see [this pan](http://share.gigapan.org/viewGigapan.php?id=18071) along with some of my other efforts [on my Gigapan home page](http://share.gigapan.org/viewProfile.php?userid=1737). What I think is really 'cool' - and there is no other word for it I am afraid - is the ability to view these panoramas in [Google Earth](http://earth.google.com/).
