Today I start a new job at the [Natural History Museum](http://www.nhm.ac.uk/ "Natural History Museum London") London. For the next two years I'll be working on [Pan European Species Infrastructure](http://www.eu-nomen.eu/pesi/ "PESI") as the project officer for Work Package 4 - which is all about standards for exchange of taxonomic data between databases in Europe. I'll still be keeping an eye on the [Biodiversity Collections Index](http://www.biodiversitycollectionsindex.org/ "BCI") in my spare time - especially encouraging others to become involved in developing it. The two subjects overlap considerably.
