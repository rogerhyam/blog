Very inspiring. Shintoism/animism is important to me but so lost in our Scottish culture where gods of nature seem to have been shrunk to faery folk.

Somewhat disappointed by Nobuyuki's desire for posterity. For me the power of the analogue image today is that it is [potentially ephemeral](http://www.hyam.net/blog/archives/4013). Also the fact that he has a digital step in his workflow freaks me a little bit - but that is just of where I am in developing my work process.
