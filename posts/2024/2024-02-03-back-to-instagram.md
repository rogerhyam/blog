I gave up on social media (other than commenting on YouTube videos) a few years ago but have decided I need to loosen up a bit. I still feel the great animosity towards the social media platforms. They are not a benign force. But it is hard to "kick against the pricks" (Acts 5:9 & also Johnny Cash). I've decided to start posting again but on my own terms. I'll only post what I have already shared here. I'll post scans of prints not digital work. I do have a naive feeling that things might be opening up with Meta-Threads starting to support [ActivityPub](https://www.w3.org/TR/activitypub/) federation. We will see on that one! [Visit my Instagram here](https://www.instagram.com/rogerhyam).
