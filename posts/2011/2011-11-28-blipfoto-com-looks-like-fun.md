I have just been introduced to [blipfoto.com](http://www.blipfoto.com). A photo journal where you upload an image a day. It has re-kindled my desire to take photos. It will be interesting to see how long it keeps my interest. There appear to be quite a few people who are addicted! I have added a plug-in to the sidebar of this blog.
