I can't remember whether I have seen it before or not but I was walking in the woods by the river Tay when I came across [Feather Frost](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frost_flower) A.K.A Frost Flowers. It looked like people had left little lumps of tissue all over the woodland floor. Wonderful to see. Fascinating but not really beautiful. Difficult to photography.
