---
title: Every use of AI is disappointing
---

I asked ChatGPT to:

> replace the numbers on the number plate of this car with the word "career"

![Original car image](images/original_car_image.jpg)

After a minute of "thinking" ChatGPT produced this

![ChatGPT generated image](images/chat_gpt_generated_image.png)

If you'd asked a graphics person to do the same job would you be satisfied with the result?

- It looks like it is pasted on.
- The number plate is too white
- The bolts on the plate have disappeared
- UK number plates use the [Mandatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_%28typeface%29) typeface which is extremely different from the one used here.

After nearly four years of what is supposed to have been exponential improvement this is still Dancing Bear software. Like a bear it can be made to dance but not very well.

Every time I use AI (voluntarily or not) I'm disappointed with the results and I think most of the world agrees with me.
