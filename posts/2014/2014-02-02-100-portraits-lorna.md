[![Lorna](images/Lorna-426x640.jpg)](http://www.hyam.net/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/Lorna.jpg)

I had liked the light in Lorna's office when I had been in for meetings but when I went in this time it was pretty dismal. I ended up getting her to stand on the window ledge (on the inside!) The next obstacle was that she kept laughing - maybe because I was asking her to do odd things. Then for the first few shots I had the camera set to 6400 ISO - and of course the best shot was one of these - hence the gritty look. But I'm actually pretty pleased with the result! Victory from the jaws of defeat?
