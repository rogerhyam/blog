Had a lovely couple of hours doing the [Pedal On Parliament](http://pedalonparliament.org/) ride today and took the opportunity to spend the time photographing people. Everyone was in a good mood and, although there were a few people who didn't want their photos taken, most were up for it. I had a couple of out of focus shots and one of a friend who was pulling such a silly face I couldn't bring myself to share it! The resulting acceptable 28 portraits are below.
