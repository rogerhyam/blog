Stuck inside today but snapped this using my 110mm cassegrain reflector and Fujifilm X-M1 through open living room window in the centre of Edinburgh. I could see the waves of warm air rippling the image but I wanted to see if I could combine the camera and telescope and they make a really nice little unit. The manual focus peaking on the camera is brilliant for this.
