Last year Channel 4 declared Edinburgh the [best place to live in the UK](http://www.channel4.com/4homes/on-tv/best-and-worst/edinburgh-the-best-place-to-live-in-the-uk-08-06-03_p_1.html) according to a comparison of facilities, house prices and jobs etc.

But today the BBC news website [reports a study](http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/health/7584321.stm) that says it is the least cheerful place in the UK.

Does this mean living in the best place makes people sad - probably.
