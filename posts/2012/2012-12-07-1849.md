Stumbled on this and really liked it:

> The opposite of love is not hate, it's indifference.
> 
> The opposite of art is not ugliness, it's indifference.
> 
> The opposite of faith is not heresy, it's indifference.
> 
> And the opposite of life is not death, it's indifference.

[Elie Wiesel](http://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Elie_Wiesel) (1986)

This chimes with my practice. It is easy to take the equanimity of Buddhism _(upekkhā)_ to be a kind of laid back indifference when the opposite is the case.  The combination of the four Brahmavihara (loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy and equanimity) plug us into experiencing life to the full.
