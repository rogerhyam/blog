I have been on core staff at the [Royal Botanic Garden Edinburgh](http://www.rbge.org.uk/) for a year now and, as part of my role there, have established a Wordpress blog for the institution. This will act as a combined blogging platform for everyone associated with the organisation as well as a more general tool for gathering information about points of interest within the gardens. The site is called [Botanics Stories](http://stories.rbge.org.uk/). If you are interested in biodiversity or horticulture I urge you to check it out and [add its feed to your RSS reader](http://stories.rbge.org.uk/feed).

From now on any biodiversity related blogging I do will be at Botanics Stories. You can [follow my latest blogs there](http://stories.rbge.org.uk/archives/author/rhyam) or [add the feed to your reader](http://stories.rbge.org.uk/archives/author/rhyam/feed).

This hyam.net/blog will now focus more on my personal stuff - mainly [Mindfulness](http://www.hyam.net/blog/archives/category/mindfulness_buddhism), [Photography](http://www.hyam.net/blog/archives/category/photography) and occasionally "[truly pathetic verbiage](http://www.hyam.net/blog/archives/category/misc)" .
